# PART 3: Teacher & Counselor Feedback & Evaluation Framework
**Purpose:** Structured counselor/teacher evaluation evaluating behavioral competencies, habits, strengths, career interest areas, and freeform qualitative observations.  
**Total Questions:** 16 Questions across 3 Sections.

---

### Section A: Behavioral & Competency Ratings (1–5 Scale)
All rating questions use a 6-option scale:
* `1`: **Very Low**
* `2`: **Low**
* `3`: **Average**
* `4`: **Good**
* `5`: **Very Good**
* `N/O`: **Not Observed**

Each question displays side-by-side student self-reported responses or teacher guidance criteria to assist the educator:

#### 1. Question `tf1` *(Required)*
* **Prompt:** **How well do the student's reported interests match what you have observed?**
* **Type:** 1–5 Rating Scale + `N/O`
* **Cross-Referenced Student Data:** Student's answers to Q1 (Curiosity Subjects), Q2 (Joyful Activities), Q6 (Career Paths), and Q7 (Dream Role).

#### 2. Question `tf2` *(Required)*
* **Prompt:** **How well do the student's reported strengths match your observations?**
* **Type:** 1–5 Rating Scale + `N/O`
* **Cross-Referenced Student Data:** Student's answers to Q3 (Self-reported Strengths & Talents).

#### 3. Question `tf3` *(Required)*
* **Prompt:** **How would you rate the student's problem-solving ability?**
* **Type:** 1–5 Rating Scale + `N/O`
* **Cross-Referenced Student Data:** Objective score on the 15-question Aptitude Assessment & answer to Q5 (Challenge Approach).

#### 4. Question `tf4` *(Required)*
* **Prompt:** **How would you rate the student's ability to learn independently?**
* **Type:** 1–5 Rating Scale + `N/O`
* **Cross-Referenced Student Data:** Student's answers to Q4 (Work Environment) & Q5 (Challenge Approach).

#### 5. Question `tf5` *(Required)*
* **Prompt:** **How would you rate the student's teamwork?**
* **Type:** 1–5 Rating Scale + `N/O`
* **Cross-Referenced Student Data:** Student's answers to Q4 (Work Environment) & Q3 (Strengths - Teamwork).

#### 6. Question `tf6` *(Required)*
* **Prompt:** **How would you rate the student's communication?**
* **Type:** 1–5 Rating Scale + `N/O`
* **Cross-Referenced Student Data:** Student's answers to Q3 (Communication) & Q2 (Sharing Ideas & Storytelling).

#### 7. Question `tf7` *(Required)*
* **Prompt:** **How would you rate the student's persistence when facing difficult tasks?**
* **Type:** 1–5 Rating Scale + `N/O`
* **Cross-Referenced Student Data:** Student's answers to Q5 (Perseverance Approach) & Q9 (Optimism & Readiness).

#### 8. Question `tf_sincerity` *(Required)*
* **Prompt:** **How would you rate the student's sincerity and dedication towards learning and duties?**
* **Type:** 1–5 Rating Scale + `N/O`
* **Educator Observation Focus Criteria:**
  * Genuine effort and earnestness in class assignments and projects.
  * Honesty, authenticity, and taking personal ownership of learning.
  * Sustained focus without requiring continuous teacher intervention.

#### 9. Question `tf_attendance` *(Required)*
* **Prompt:** **How would you rate the student's attendance, punctuality, and regularity?**
* **Type:** 1–5 Rating Scale + `N/O`
* **Educator Observation Focus Criteria:**
  * Regularity in class attendance with minimal unexcused absences.
  * Arriving on time for classes, laboratory sessions, and group activities.
  * Timely submission of homework, projects, and lab reports.

#### 10. Question `tf_discipline` *(Required)*
* **Prompt:** **How would you rate the student's obedience and classroom discipline?**
* **Type:** 1–5 Rating Scale + `N/O`
* **Educator Observation Focus Criteria:**
  * Adherence to school policies, classroom decorum, and instructions.
  * Maintaining self-control during independent work and group activities.
  * Respectful, non-disruptive behavior towards teachers and classmates.

#### 11. Question `tf_respect` *(Required)*
* **Prompt:** **How would you rate the student's level of respect towards teachers, staff, and peers?**
* **Type:** 1–5 Rating Scale + `N/O`
* **Educator Observation Focus Criteria:**
  * Polite tone, active listening, and courteous speech with teachers and staff.
  * Openness to constructive feedback, advice, and guidance.
  * Empathy, inclusion, and kindness towards peers of all backgrounds.

#### 12. Question `tf_cleanliness` *(Required)*
* **Prompt:** **How would you rate the student's personal cleanliness, neatness, and care for belongings?**
* **Type:** 1–5 Rating Scale + `N/O`
* **Educator Observation Focus Criteria:**
  * Keeping study desk, laboratory bench, and workspace organized.
  * Careful handling and neat presentation of books, notebooks, and equipment.
  * Personal hygiene, tidy uniform/attire, and pride in neat work.

# PART 4: Student Registration & Profile Demographic Fields
**Purpose:** Initial intake data filled by the teacher when enrolling a student into the platform roster.

| Field Name | Type / Format | Options / Values | Required? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Student Full Name** | Text input | Any valid full name | Yes |
| **Date of Birth** | Date picker (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | Calendar date | Yes |
| **Class Grade** | Dropdown / Select | `8`, `9`, `10`, `11`, `12` | Yes |
| **School Name** | Text input | School institution name | Optional |
| **Parent / Guardian Occupation** | Text input | e.g., Agriculture, Small Business, Service, Healthcare... | Optional |
| **Family Income Range** | Text input / Select | Contextual income bracket | Optional |
| **Unique Student Access Code** | System-generated 8-character string | e.g., `STU-7A8B9C` | Automatic (Primary Student Key) |
